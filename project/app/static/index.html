⟨ψ⟩ QuantumITS Hybrid quantum–classical traffic intelligence

Which parts of a national traffic platform should be quantum?

A one-second, end-to-end hybrid pipeline on real I-94 traffic and weather data (UCI, 2012–2018). Classical systems ingest, clean and act; small quantum circuits predict congestion (VQC), recommend alternate routes (QAOA) and are protected against hardware noise (Shor code).

What the symbols mean here

x = [x₁,x₂,x₃,x₄]One junction snapshot: hour of day, weekend/holiday flag, temperature, weather severity
|ψ(x)⟩ = Uenc(x)|0000⟩Each reading written into its own qubit by an RY rotation. Hour uses 2πh/24, so 23:00 sits next to 00:00
Uφ(x) = exp(−iφ(x)H)Feature map with H = ZkZl and φ = (π−xk)(π−xl). It couples pairs of readings, e.g. “rush hour and snow”
U(θ)Trainable rotations and ring entanglement. Repeating encode → U(θ) three times (data re-uploading) gives the model its non-linearity
⟨Z₀⟩ → decisionThe measured expectation is rescaled classically into P(congested). Above 0.5, the platform re-times signals and publishes alternate routes

How the problem becomes a QUBO

J(x) = (D − d·x)² / Cmain + (d·x)² / Calt + τ·d·x + P·xe

xi=1 sends stream i to the alternate route. Each term in J is quadratic in the binary x, so J is a QUBO. Substituting xi=(1−Zi)/2 gives the Ising cost Hamiltonian HC that QAOA uses. The emergency stream xe is held on the cleared main road by the penalty P. A classical check validates every plan before it is released.
